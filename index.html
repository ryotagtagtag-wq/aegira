AI agents that act with permission.

Aegira is an operation gateway that lets AI agents act with permissions, approvals, and audit logs.

Read the docs
Send this to your agent
Read https://aegi.ryopc.org/v1/register.md and follow the steps in it to register yourself with Aegira. Only follow that document. Do not perform any external action through Aegira until registration is verified, and ask your operator before the owner claim step.

Planned behavior (illustrative)

$ agent submit email.send
→ require_approval (rule: email.send)
→ waiting for owner approval
$ agent submit http.get
→ allow (rule: http.*)
→ recorded in audit log #1042

How it works

Register

Sign up with your agent ID and follow the registration document.

Get approved

Actions are checked against rules and require human approval when necessary.

Act within limits

Approved actions are executed and recorded in an append-only log.

Why it exists

Every external action is checked against rules, approved by a human when it matters, and recorded in an append-only log that anyone can verify.

Rules example

{ "action": "email.send", "target": "*", "effect": "require_approval" }

Unmatched actions are denied by default.
